About

Who I am and how I like to work.

I'm a backend-leaning full-stack engineer who has spent most of the last eight years as the person responsible for how a product's server side is designed, built and kept healthy.

At Jetmanlabs I was the founding engineer, choosing the stack and architecture for an API-testing platform and its Electron desktop client. Since 2022 I've led the backend of Truck Parking Club, a two-sided marketplace where property owners list truck parking and drivers and fleets book it — hourly to monthly, with dynamic pricing, role-based organisations and Stripe underneath everything.

The lead part of the job is what I enjoy most: running feasibility studies for upcoming features, turning vague requirements into scoped deliverables with product and design, and making architecture calls that the team can live with for years. I care about clean schemas, sensible failure modes, and code that's boring in all the right places.

Outside work I read a lot, tinker with developer tools, and I'm currently deep in AI agents and LLM integrations — wiring conversational agents into real production systems rather than demos.
